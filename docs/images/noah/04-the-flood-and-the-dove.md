# Noah lesson 4: The flood and the dove (Genesis 7:17–8:19)

One image per story card in `src/lib/content/bible-stories/noah/04-the-flood-and-the-dove.ts`. The narration is each card's text, so it isn't repeated here. Run `python3 docs/images/tools/build-lesson.py docs/images/noah/04-the-flood-and-the-dove.md` to get every prompt in [04-the-flood-and-the-dove.prompts.md](04-the-flood-and-the-dove.prompts.md).

**Arc of the lesson:** only the ark is left on the water → but God remembered Noah → the dove and the olive leaf → out onto dry ground. The light starts in **Judgment**, where lesson 3 ends, and turns through **Grace** to **Calm**.

Judgment is shown only by empty water: nothing floats in it, and no people or animals are anywhere outside the ark until the last image.

### 1. `waters-prevailed`: Judgment

**Light:** JUDGMENT
**Camera:** WIDE
**Recipes:** Water, Clouds
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the ark floats small and alone on an endless calm grey sea under a low, heavy sky. Far to one side, only the very tip of a mountain peak still shows above the water. Nothing else is in the water.
**Note:** The emptiness is the point: no debris, no bodies, no animals, no other boats.

### 2. `god-remembered`: Grace

**Light:** GRACE
**Camera:** WIDE
**Recipes:** Water, Clouds
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the ark rests on a rocky mountain slope, tilted slightly, as the water slowly draws back below it. A strong wind ripples across the water, and more rocky peaks rise out of the sea around it. Above, the heavy clouds are breaking apart.
**Note:** The wind is shown only by ripples and moving clouds, with no shapes or faces in them.

### 3. `raven-and-dove`: Grace

**Light:** GRACE
**Camera:** CLOSE
**Recipes:** Faces, Animals
**Characters:** NOAH
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, noah.png
**Shows:** In the centre, Noah leans out of a small square wooden window high in the ark's side, his hand stretched out as a white dove lands on it, a small fresh green olive leaf in her beak. His face is full of quiet joy. Far behind, over the shining water, a black raven flies away.
**Note:** The olive leaf must be clearly visible and green. The raven stays small and far away.

### 4. `go-out`: Calm

**Light:** CALM
**Camera:** MEDIUM
**Recipes:** Faces, Animals
**Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, noah.png, ark.png

Beside them, pairs of animals step out onto the grass and birds fly up into a clear sky.
**Note:** Eight people in all. Check Noah's wife against characters.md (terracotta-red head covering).
