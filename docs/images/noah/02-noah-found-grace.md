# Noah lesson 2: Noah found grace (Genesis 6:8–22)

One image per story card in `src/lib/content/bible-stories/noah/02-noah-found-grace.ts`. The narration is each card's text, so it isn't repeated here. Run `python3 docs/images/tools/build-lesson.py docs/images/noah/02-noah-found-grace.md` to get every prompt in [02-noah-found-grace.prompts.md](02-noah-found-grace.prompts.md).

**Arc of the lesson:** one man in the dark finds grace → he walks with God, with his family → God gives him the plan → he obeys. The light moves from **Night** (picking up from lesson 1's last image) through **Calm** to **Grace**.

This is the first lesson with Noah in it. When the first good Noah appears, save a clean, close crop of him as `docs/images/anchors/noah.png` and attach it for every later scene. Do the same for the finished ark in scene 4 (`ark.png`): lessons 3 to 5 need it.

### 2. `walked-with-god`: Calm

**Light:** CALM
**Camera:** MEDIUM
**Recipes:** Faces, Trees, Animals
**Characters:** NOAH, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, noah.png
**Shows:** In the centre, Noah walks along a path through a green barley field with his three sons beside him as one group: Shem carries a wooden plough on his shoulder, Ham carries a sheep across his shoulders, and Japheth carries a clay water jar. They talk together and smile. Behind them, the small mud-brick house with its olive tree, and open fields beyond.
**Note:** Check each son against characters.md: the tunic colours (indigo, mustard, olive green) are the quickest way to tell them apart.

### 3. `build-an-ark`: Calm

**Light:** CALM
**Camera:** MEDIUM
**Recipes:** Faces, Trees
**Characters:** NOAH, THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, noah.png
**Shows:** In the centre, Noah stretches a knotted measuring cord along a long plank laid on wooden trestles, while two of his sons lift a beam behind him. Behind them rises the ark under construction on dry land among olive trees, far from any water: its lower half is planked and dark with pitch, its upper wooden ribs still bare against the sky. Wood shavings and a clay pot of pitch on the ground.
**Note:** No crowd and no mocking onlookers: the text doesn't mention them. The ark must already look box-shaped, with no bow and no mast.
