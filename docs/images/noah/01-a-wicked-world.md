# Noah lesson 1: A wicked world (Genesis 6:1–7)

Save the images to `static/media/bible-stories/noah/a-wicked-world/`, and the voice-overs to the same folder with the same names (`.mp3`).

**Arc of the lesson:** life spreads → sin spreads → God sees the heart → God is grieved and judgment is coming → but God is patient, and one light still burns. The mood moves from **Calm** through **Shadow** to **Judgment**, and ends on a small note of **Grace**.

No named characters appear in this lesson, so no character lines are needed.

### 1. `world-filling-up`: Calm

**Narration:** "After Adam and Eve sinned, their children spread across the earth. Families grew, villages grew, and there were more and more people."

**Shows:** A wide, fertile river valley. A winding river runs from the lower left into the distance. Small clusters of flat-roofed mud-brick houses line both banks, and more villages are scattered over the hills toward the horizon. Smoke rises from cooking fires. Green and gold fields, small flocks of sheep and a few tiny people working the fields. On the right, new houses are still being built.

### 2. `sin-spread`: Shadow

**Narration:** "But sin spread with them. People grabbed what wasn't theirs, cheated their neighbours, and turned away anyone in need."

**Shows:** A crowded market street. In the centre, a greedy merchant presses down on one pan of a bronze balance scale with two fingers and pulls a customer's bag of silver toward himself, while the customer reaches out in dismay. On the left, a young man tears a basket of bread from an old man's arms, and loaves spill on the ground. On the right, a richly dressed man slams a door on a ragged mother holding a crying child and an empty bowl. Behind them, people turn their backs.

### 3. `only-evil`: Shadow

**Narration:** "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."
**Caption ref:** Genesis 6:5

**Shows:** On a flat rooftop, two men embrace like old friends. The man on the left is seen from behind. The man facing us looks over his friend's shoulder with a cold, scheming face, and his hand, held low and out of his friend's sight, grips a rough stone. Behind them, the town spreads out with lamps in its windows.

### 4. `the-lord-was-grieved`: Judgment

**Narration:** "Sin isn't a small thing to God. It grieved him to his heart. He said he would blot out man from the face of the land."
**Caption ref:** Genesis 6:6–7

**Shows:** An empty open-air potter's workshop on a low hill. In the centre, on a potter's wheel that has stopped, a tall, finely shaped clay jar with painted bands has a deep crack from rim to base and has slumped to one side. A broken shard lies at its foot. On a low shelf beside it stand two or three smaller cracked jars, and a cloth hangs over an empty stool. Below and behind are the town and river from images 1–3. No people, no storm clouds, no rain.

### 5. `one-light`: Judgment → Grace

**Narration:** "But God is patient. He gave the world a hundred and twenty years of warning. And in that dark world, there was one man who still walked with God."

**Shows:** A dark town spread across the lower part of the picture, with only a few lit windows. On a low hill on the right, a little apart from the town, stands a single small mud-brick house with an olive tree beside it. One window glows gold and casts a soft pool of light on the grass. Above, the clouds part slightly to show a few stars.

### Tips for this lesson

- Make image 1 first and use it as the **style reference** for images 2–5, so the valley, the houses and the paper texture stay consistent.
- Images 4 and 5 both show the same town below, so they read as the same place. If the generator supports it, use image 4 as an image reference for 5.
- Image 4 must have **no person** in it. If the generator adds a potter, a hand, a face in the sky or a glowing figure, generate it again: we don't picture God. The jar stands for what God made with care (Genesis 2:7: he formed man from the dust), so it has to be clearly beautiful as well as clearly broken.
- Image 3 depends on one contrast: the warm embrace against the cold face and the hidden stone. If the generator turns the face into a smile, hides the hand, or puts the stone in a raised hand, generate it again. Don't accept a softer version.
