# Noah lesson 3: Into the ark (Genesis 7:1–16)

One image per story card in `src/lib/content/bible-stories/noah/03-into-the-ark.ts`. The narration is each card's text, so it isn't repeated here. Run `python3 docs/images/tools/build-lesson.py docs/images/noah/03-into-the-ark.md` to get every prompt in [03-into-the-ark.prompts.md](03-into-the-ark.prompts.md).

**Arc of the lesson:** God calls Noah in → the flood begins → God himself shuts the door, and inside is safe. The light moves from **Calm** (a last clear day, with one dark cloud far away) into **Judgment**, with the warm glow of the ark's windows as the only warmth at the end.

The ark is in every image, so attach `ark.png` from lesson 2 each time. No people are ever shown outside the ark once the rain starts.

### 1. `go-in`: Calm

**Light:** CALM
**Camera:** WIDE
**Recipes:** Animals, Trees
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the finished ark on its green hill, its large side door open and a wooden ramp leading down to the grass. Pairs of animals walk calmly up the ramp in a line: two elephants, two camels, two sheep, with pairs of birds flying in overhead. Beside the foot of the ramp, small, Noah's family of eight waits with baskets and bundles. Far away on the horizon, one low dark cloud.
**Note:** The animals walk in pairs and stay calm, no crowding. Keep the family small: they are not the subject here.

### 2. `flood-begins`: Judgment

**Light:** JUDGMENT
**Camera:** WIDE
**Recipes:** Rain / storm, Water, Clouds
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the ark stands closed on its hill while heavy rain pours down from a low sky. Around the foot of the hill, water bursts up from cracks in the ground in low fountains and spreads out into wide, rising streams across the valley. Nothing else stands in the water.
**Note:** No people and no animals outside the ark, and nothing floating in the water. Judgment is shown by weather and emptiness only.

### 3. `shut-in`: Judgment

**Light:** JUDGMENT
**Camera:** OBJECT
**Recipes:** Rain / storm, Glowing light
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the great side door of the ark, shut tight, its wooden ramp lifted against it and its edges sealed with dark pitch. Rain streams down the planks. Above the door, warm lamplight glows from the narrow row of small openings under the roof. No person, no hand and no figure anywhere near the door.
**Note:** The card says God shut the door, so no one may be seen closing it: reject any hand, figure, beam or glow on the door itself. The only warmth is the lamplight inside.

### Poster

**Title:** The LORD Shut Him In.
**Light:** JUDGMENT
**Camera:** WIDE
**Recipes:** Rain / storm, Glowing light, Clouds
**Characters:** THE ARK
**Anchors:** style-anchor.png, ark.png
**Shows:** In the centre, the closed ark stands on its hill under a heavy, dark sky with a soft veil of rain, its row of small openings under the roof glowing warm gold. The hill below is empty. The lower fifth of the image is calm, simple dark grass with nothing in it.
**Note:** "LORD" must be in capitals, as in the ESV. If Grok can't spell it, use version B and add the title yourself.
