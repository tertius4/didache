# Noah and the Flood: video script for the six story segments

> **Superseded for visuals:** lessons now use narrated still images. The style, rules and character sheet are in [docs/art/style-guide.md](../art/style-guide.md), and the per-lesson prompts are in `docs/art/noah/`. The narration lines below are still a useful starting point.

This file has one short video per lesson. Each video replaces the story cards at the start of that lesson. The "Learn more" doctrine sheets stay as optional text.

---

## 1. How to make the videos

1. **Make one clip per shot.** Grok Imagine makes short clips (about 6–10 seconds). For each shot below, paste into Grok:
   **STYLE BLOCK** + the **CHARACTER** lines for the people in that shot + the shot's **VISUAL PROMPT**.
   Paste the full style block every time. Grok doesn't remember earlier clips, so this keeps the look consistent.
2. **Make clips without speech.** The style block asks Grok for ambient sound only. Record the narration separately (your own voice or one TTS voice) so every lesson has the same narrator.
3. **Assemble each lesson in an editor** (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, etc.):
   - Put the clips in order, then add the narration track.
   - Add the on-screen captions listed per shot. Bible verses should always appear as on-screen text. Don't let Grok draw text; AI video garbles letters.
   - Add a soft music bed (see the audio notes in the style block) at about −20 dB under the narration.
   - End with a 1-second hold on the last frame.
4. **Export settings:** vertical **9:16, 1080×1920, MP4 (H.264), 30 fps**, about 60–90 seconds per lesson. Name the files after the lesson ids, e.g. `a-wicked-world.mp4`.
5. **Regenerate a clip if:**
   - Noah's family looks different from the character sheet.
   - Any text appears in the frame.
   - Anything breaks the content rules below.

## 2. Content rules (important for Reformed teaching)

- **Never depict God** in any visible form: no face, figure, hands, glowing person, or eye in the clouds. God's presence and speech are shown only through the narration and through people's reactions. Wind in the grass is fine as a backdrop, never as "God". (Heidelberg Catechism Q&A 96–98.)
- **Never depict Jesus.** When the narration mentions Christ, show landscape, a road toward a sunrise, or the ark.
- **Family-friendly:**
  - No dead bodies and no drowning people.
  - No blood. Violence is shown only as pushing or arguing.
  - No nudity. Noah's tent in lesson 6 is only ever seen from outside.
- **No text, letters, logos or watermarks** in the generated video.
- **Historically appropriate people:** Ancient Near Eastern, with olive-to-brown skin and dark hair. Simple woven tunics and robes in earth tones. No modern objects.

## 3. STYLE BLOCK (paste at the start of every prompt)

```
STYLE: Warm, playful 2D animated children's-book style, like a modern educational app. Flat shapes with soft rounded edges, subtle paper texture, gentle soft shadows, no harsh outlines. Friendly characters with simple round faces, large expressive eyes, small rounded noses. Smooth, gentle animation; slow, calm camera moves (slow push-ins, gentle pans). Bright but soft colour palette: cream background tones (#FFFAF0), leafy green (#2FB36B), sky blue (#3AA6E8), sunny yellow (#FFC533), warm wood brown (#A8693C), soft purple (#8A63D2) accents. Skies are light blue with puffy rounded clouds; water is layered rounded waves in two blues. Vertical 9:16 framing with the main action in the centre and middle third of the frame. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks anywhere. Family-friendly, wholesome, no violence, no gore, no nudity. AUDIO: ambient natural sound only (wind, birds, rain, water, animals, wood creaking). No dialogue, no narration, no singing, no music.
```

## 4. CHARACTER SHEET (paste the lines for characters who appear in a shot)

```
NOAH: Ancient Near Eastern man, olive-brown skin, kind wrinkled eyes, long grey-white beard, bushy grey eyebrows, cream-coloured wool robe with a brown sash, brown head wrap, sturdy sandals, wooden walking staff. Gentle, steady, faithful expression.

NOAH'S WIFE: Ancient Near Eastern woman, olive-brown skin, warm smile, grey hair under a terracotta-red head covering, long sand-coloured dress with a red woven belt.

SHEM: Young adult man, olive-brown skin, short dark beard, calm thoughtful face, deep blue tunic.

HAM: Young adult man, brown skin, clean-shaven, curly black hair, restless grin, mustard-yellow tunic.

JAPHETH: Young adult man, light olive skin, short dark curly hair, thin moustache, leafy-green tunic.

SONS' WIVES: Three young women, olive-brown skin, head coverings in soft purple, sky blue and peach, simple long dresses.

THE ARK: Huge rectangular wooden boat of warm brown planks with horizontal darker plank lines, a long wooden house on top with a sloped roof, one large side door and small square windows glowing warm yellow.
```

## 5. Lesson scripts

Each shot lists:

- **Duration**
- **Characters**: which character lines to paste
- **Visual prompt**: paste after the style block and characters
- **Narration (VO)**: record separately
- **Caption**: add in the editor

Verses are from the ESV.

---

### Lesson 1: A wicked world (Genesis 6:1–7), about 40 seconds

**Shot 1.1 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** Wide establishing shot of a green valley at the dawn of the world, rounded hills, a river. Time-lapse: small clay-brick villages appear and spread across the hills, smoke rising from chimneys, tiny people walking between houses. Slow pan from left to right.
- **VO:** "After Adam and Eve sinned, their children spread across the earth. There were more and more people…"
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 1.2 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** A busy ancient town street at dusk. Townspeople arguing with angry faces; one man grabs a basket of bread from another and runs; a merchant cheats with a tilted scale; a door is slammed in a beggar's face. No blood, no weapons. Colours become duller and greyer as the shot goes on. Slow push-in.
- **VO:** "…but sin spread with them. People weren't just doing bad things now and then. Their hearts were turned away from God."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 1.3 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** Low-angle view of the same town under a darkening sky; heavy grey rounded clouds slowly gather and swirl overhead, light dims, the warm colours drain away. Calm, solemn mood. Slow tilt up toward the clouds. The clouds are just clouds, with no faces or shapes in them.
- **VO:** "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 6:5_, followed by the verse text

**Shot 1.4 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** Night. Wide view across dark rooftops of a large town under stormy clouds. Camera slowly drifts toward the edge of town, where one small house on a hill has a single warm yellow glowing window. Gentle wind. Hopeful, quiet ending.
- **VO:** "Sin isn't a small thing to God. It grieved him to his heart, and judgment was coming. But God is patient… and there was one man who walked with him."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 6:6_

### Lesson 2: Noah found grace (Genesis 6:8–22), about 50 seconds

**Shot 2.1 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH
- **Visual prompt:** Night under a sky full of stars. Outside the small house with the glowing window, Noah kneels on the grass with hands folded, praying peacefully. Behind him, in the distance, the dark town. Slow push-in on Noah's calm face.
- **VO:** "In a world full of evil, one small word changes everything: 'But.' But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 6:8_: "But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD."

**Shot 2.2 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH
- **Visual prompt:** Bright morning. Noah's family works together in a green field beside their home: Noah's wife carries a water jar, Shem ploughs, Ham carries a sheep, Japheth waves. Warm smiles. Gentle pan across the family from left to right.
- **VO:** "Noah was a righteous man. Noah walked with God. He had three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 2.3 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH
- **Visual prompt:** Noah alone in a field of tall grass at sunrise. A soft wind moves through the grass. Noah stops, looks up, listening intently; his eyes widen with awe and seriousness. No figure, no beam of light, nothing visible in the sky except clouds. Slow push-in on Noah.
- **VO:** "God told Noah that a great flood was coming. And he gave him a plan: 'Make yourself an ark of gopher wood.'"
- **Caption:** _Genesis 6:14_

**Shot 2.4 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Building montage on dry land far from any sea: Noah and his sons saw logs, lift huge planks, hammer pegs; the wooden ribs of an enormous boat frame rise taller than the trees; Noah measures with a knotted cord; the sons brush dark pitch over the planks. In the background a few townspeople point and laugh. Time-lapse feeling, cheerful energy.
- **VO:** "It was huge, about one hundred and thirty-five metres long, with rooms inside, and sealed with pitch inside and out. And there was no water anywhere near!"
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 2.5 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Golden sunset. The completed ark stands enormous on a green hill. Noah stands small in front of it, wiping his brow, smiling with quiet joy, leaning on his staff. Camera slowly pulls back to show the full size of the ark against the sky.
- **VO:** "Noah believed God, even though no flood had ever been seen. Noah did this; he did all that God commanded him."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 6:22_

---

### Lesson 3: Into the ark (Genesis 7:1–16), about 50 seconds

**Shot 3.1 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** The ark on a green hill, its large side door open with a wooden ramp. From the surrounding hills, animals walk calmly toward it in pairs: elephants, giraffes, lions, sheep, camels, rabbits, with birds flying in pairs overhead. Peaceful parade, soft morning light. Slow crane shot rising.
- **VO:** "God said to Noah, 'Go into the ark, you and all your household.' The animals came: seven pairs of every clean animal, and a pair of all the others."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 7:1_

**Shot 3.2 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES, THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Noah's family of eight walks up the ramp into the ark carrying baskets and bundles. In the background, townspeople feast at a long table, laughing and drinking, not looking at the ark at all. The sky is clear but a faint dark cloud sits on the horizon.
- **VO:** "Everyone else carried on as usual: eating, drinking, celebrating. They didn't listen."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 3.3 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Close-up exterior of the ark's huge wooden side door. The ramp lifts and the great door slowly swings shut on its own, with no person or hands visible, closing with a deep heavy thud that shakes dust from the planks. Hold on the closed door.
- **VO:** "Once everyone was inside, Noah didn't close the door. And the LORD shut him in."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 7:16_: "And the LORD shut him in."

**Shot 3.4 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES
- **Visual prompt:** Inside the ark: a warm, lamp-lit wooden room with stalls of calm animals. The family sits together. The first raindrops begin to drum on the roof above; everyone looks up, then Noah's wife takes Noah's hand. Cosy, safe feeling. Slow push-in.
- **VO:** "Seven days later, the rain began. Noah was six hundred years old."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 3.5 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Exterior, wide shot: heavy rain pours from dark rounded clouds; fountains of water burst up from cracks in the ground; streams become rivers and the water rises around the ark until it gently lifts and floats. No people or animals outside. Dramatic but not frightening.
- **VO:** "Water burst up from deep under the earth and poured down from the sky. It rained for forty days and forty nights."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 7:11–12_

---

### Lesson 4: The flood and the dove (Genesis 7:17–8:19), about 60 seconds

**Shot 4.1 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** The ark floats alone on an endless calm grey ocean under an overcast sky. In the far distance, the very tip of a mountain peak slowly sinks beneath the water. No people, no animals, nothing else in the water. Quiet, lonely, solemn mood. Very slow pan.
- **VO:** "The water rose until even the highest mountains were covered. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 7:23_

**Shot 4.2 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** The grey clouds above the ark begin to break apart; a strong wind ripples across the water; waves calm; warm sunlight breaks through and the sea turns from grey to bright blue. Hopeful mood. Camera tilts down from the sky to the ark.
- **VO:** "But God remembered Noah. He made a wind blow over the earth, and the waters began to go down."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:1_: "But God remembered Noah."

**Shot 4.3 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** The ark resting on a rocky mountain slope. Around it, the water slowly drains away, revealing more mountain peaks rising out of the sea. Sunny sky. Slow pull-back.
- **VO:** "The ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:4_

**Shot 4.4 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH
- **Visual prompt:** Noah opens a small wooden window high on the ark and releases a black raven, which flies off over the water. Then he gently releases a white dove from his hands. Later, the tired dove flies back and lands on his outstretched hand, and he lifts her inside.
- **VO:** "Noah sent out a raven, and then a dove. But the dove found nowhere to land, and she came back."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 4.5 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE
- **Visual prompt:** Evening, golden light. The white dove flies toward the ark window holding a small fresh green olive leaf in her beak. Noah reaches out, she lands on his hand, he takes the leaf and his face fills with joy; Noah's wife leans in beside him, smiling. Close-up.
- **VO:** "A week later, she came back in the evening, with a fresh olive leaf! The next time, she didn't come back at all."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:11_

**Shot 4.6 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES, THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** The great ark door is open. Noah's family and pairs of animals step out onto fresh green grass on the mountainside under a bright blue sky; birds fly out, rabbits hop, the family stretches and breathes the fresh air. Joyful. Slow crane up and back.
- **VO:** "Noah waited for God's word. When God said, 'Go out from the ark,' Noah, his family, and all the animals stepped onto dry ground."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:16_

---

### Lesson 5: The rainbow covenant (Genesis 8:20–9:17), about 50 seconds

**Shot 5.1 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES
- **Visual prompt:** On the green mountainside, Noah stacks rounded grey stones into a simple altar. A small fire burns on top and a soft column of smoke rises into the clear sky. The family kneels around it, heads bowed in worship. The fire shows no animal detail. Reverent, peaceful.
- **VO:** "The very first thing Noah did on dry ground was worship. He built an altar and offered sacrifices to the LORD."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:20_

**Shot 5.2 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** Time-lapse of one valley with a small farm, cycling through the seasons: spring seeds being sown in brown soil, green shoots, a golden wheat harvest, autumn leaves, gentle snow, summer sun, then day turning into a starry night and back to day. Smooth, continuous transitions.
- **VO:** "God promised: 'While the earth remains, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night, shall not cease.'"
- **Caption:** _Genesis 8:22_

**Shot 5.3 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES
- **Visual prompt:** Noah stands on a hilltop with his family gathered around him, his arms spread wide over them in blessing, looking out over a vast green empty land waiting to be filled. Warm afternoon light. Slow pull-back.
- **VO:** "God blessed Noah and his sons: 'Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth.'"
- **Caption:** _Genesis 9:1_

**Shot 5.4 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, NOAH'S WIFE, SHEM, HAM, JAPHETH, SONS' WIVES
- **Visual prompt:** After a light rain, sunlight breaks through and a brilliant rainbow forms across the whole sky, colours appearing one by one. The family and nearby animals look up in wonder; Noah puts his arm around his wife. Shot from behind the family toward the rainbow.
- **VO:** "Then God made a covenant, a solemn promise, with Noah and every living creature: never again would a flood destroy the earth. 'I have set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be a sign of the covenant.'"
- **Caption:** _Genesis 9:13_

**Shot 5.5 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** THE ARK
- **Visual prompt:** Wide final shot: the empty ark resting on the mountain, a full rainbow arching over it, green hills below, soft clouds. Camera slowly pulls back and upward. Peaceful ending.
- **VO:** "Every rainbow reminds us: God is patient, and he always keeps his word."
- **Caption:** none

### Lesson 6: Noah's fall (Genesis 9:18–29), about 55 seconds

> Keep this lesson especially modest: the inside of the tent is never shown, and Noah is never shown undressed.

**Shot 6.1 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH
- **Visual prompt:** A sunny hillside vineyard with rows of vines heavy with purple grape clusters. Noah, a little older, tends the vines and picks a bunch of grapes, smiling. A brown family tent stands at the edge of the vineyard. Gentle pan.
- **VO:** "After the flood, Noah became a man of the soil, and he planted a vineyard."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 9:20_

**Shot 6.2 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** Evening, exterior only: Noah's brown tent with its entrance flap closed and a dim lamp glow behind the fabric. On the ground by the entrance, a clay wine jar lies tipped over with a small spill. Quiet, slightly sad mood. Slow push-in, stopping outside the tent.
- **VO:** "One day Noah drank too much wine, got drunk, and lay uncovered in his tent. The hero of the flood fell into sin."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 6.3 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** HAM, SHEM, JAPHETH
- **Visual prompt:** Exterior: Ham lifts the tent flap slightly, peeks in (the inside is not visible to the camera), then turns away with a mocking grin and hurries over to Shem and Japheth by a campfire, pointing back at the tent. Shem and Japheth look troubled.
- **VO:** "Ham saw his father's shame, and went and told his two brothers."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 6.4 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** SHEM, JAPHETH
- **Visual prompt:** Exterior of the tent, side view: Shem and Japheth hold a large folded cloak between them, resting it on both their shoulders, and walk slowly backward toward the tent entrance with their faces turned away, eyes closed. They disappear backward through the flap. The inside of the tent is not shown. Respectful, gentle mood.
- **VO:** "But Shem and Japheth took a garment, walked in backward, and covered their father without looking."
- **Caption:** _Genesis 9:23_

**Shot 6.5 (8 s)**

- **Characters:** NOAH, SHEM
- **Visual prompt:** Morning outside the tent. Noah, fully clothed in his robe, sits on a rock looking sorrowful, then places his hand on Shem's shoulder in blessing while Shem kneels. Soft sunrise light. Close-up on Noah's face.
- **VO:** "When Noah woke up, he spoke a curse on Canaan, Ham's son, and a blessing on Shem. Noah lived nine hundred and fifty years, and then he died."
- **Caption:** none

**Shot 6.6 (10 s)**

- **Characters:** none
- **Visual prompt:** A long winding road stretches from the green hills toward a glowing sunrise on the horizon, with many generations of tiny silhouetted travellers and tents along it. No single figure is in focus. Camera slowly flies forward along the road toward the light. Hopeful, uplifting ending.
- **VO:** "Even Noah needed God's grace. The flood washed the world, but it couldn't wash the human heart. But from Shem's family would come Abraham, and one day Jesus: the Saviour who never sinned."
- **Caption:** _Romans 3:23_: "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
